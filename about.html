About Us

Introduction:

The Williamson County Amateur Radio Club (WCARC), located in Texas, is an American Radio Relay League (ARRL) affiliate. WCARC unites local amateur radio operators in support of public service, science, and engineering through amateur radio. The club promotes awareness and encourages local growth of the amateur radio hobby through educational opportunities. Club specialties include contesting, digital modes, DX, public service/emergency, repeaters, HF, and VHF/UHF.

WCARC was chartered on April 26, 1979, with six members and continues today with over 100 members. The WCARC club call sign, N5TT, is managed by Tom Whiteside, N5TW, Call Sign Trustee.
