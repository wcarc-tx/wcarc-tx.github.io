Club Events

WCARC Monthly Meetings
​
 

Date/Time:  The first Thursday of every month at 7:00 p.m.  See the Events page for details. 

Location: St. Richard's Episcopal Church, Round Rock, and on Zoom (email the board for the Zoom link)

​

Monday Night "New Hams" Net

Meet us on the air each Monday night at 8:00 p.m. CT on the N5TT Repeater System for a "round robin" discussion designed to give new and experienced hams a chance to practice talking on the air, try out their equipment, or ask questions/get Elmer help.  You do not have to be a member of WCARC to join the net, all amateur operators are welcome! 

​

You can join the N5TT repeater system through any of the frequencies listed below: 

146.640 (PL tone 162.2 Hz) - located at Seton Ascension Williamson in Round Rock

145.450 (PL tone 162.2 Hz) - located in Taylor, TX

146.900 (PL tone 100.0 Hz) - located in Cedar Park, TX

444.600 (PL tone 100.0 Hz) - located in Cedar Park, TX

145.270 (PL tone 103.5 Hz) - located in Sun City, TX (Georgetown)

Join our "Reflector" 

To receive club news and updates, get Elmer help, and join in club discussion topics, join the "Reflector" - the club's email platform hosted on the groups.io platform.  You can register for a free account at https://groups.io/register.  Click "Find or Create A Group" and search for "WCARC - Williamson County Amateur Radio Club".

​​

Amateur Radio Exam Sessions

Amateur Radio Licensing Exam Sessions are held on the first Saturday of odd months, 10:00 AM, in Cedar Park, TX. To register and see what to bring, visit K5KTF's VE exam page.

​​

Upcoming dates are under Coming Up below and on the Events page.

​

There are numerous online study guides for all three license classes (Technician, General, and Extra).  Visit https://www.arrl.org/licensing-education-training for more information.
